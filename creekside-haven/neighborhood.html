The neighborhood

Montgomery, Texas — small town square, big-city access.

Montgomery has the retail and the roads it did not have five years ago, and it kept the quiet. Creekside Haven sits right between the two.

Groceries & everyday errands

The new H-E-B on TX-105 and Kroger Marketplace cover the weekly run, with Walgreens, banks and the post office along the same stretch. No 30-minute trip for milk.

Montgomery ISD

The community is zoned to Montgomery ISD, a district families move to Montgomery County for. Campus assignments are set by the district — confirm your address before signing.

Historic downtown Montgomery

The birthplace of the Texas flag, six minutes away: local restaurants, antique shops, coffee, and a walkable square that still hosts weekend markets and festivals.

Lake Conroe

Twelve minutes to the water. Boat ramps, lakeside dining, marinas and golf are all part of an ordinary Saturday out here.

Roads that actually work

TX-105 and FM 149 connect you to TX-249 (the Aggie Expressway) and I-45 without crawling through neighborhood streets — The Woodlands in about 25 minutes, Conroe in 20.

Room to breathe

Piney Woods, ranch land and state park trails start where the parking lot ends. The greenbelt and creek along the property line are yours to walk every morning.

Getting around

Drive times from the front gate.

TX-249 opened up the run south, and I-45 handles everything north. Whether you work in The Woodlands, Conroe or from the coworking bar downstairs, the commute stays reasonable.

Weekends here

What a Saturday looks like.

Morning

Coffee on the square in downtown Montgomery, then the farmers market and a walk through the antique shops before it gets warm.

Afternoon

Lake Conroe — boat ramp, lakeside patio, or nine holes at one of the courses a few minutes up the road.

Evening

Back home for the grilling pavilion and the pool, or 25 minutes down to Market Street in The Woodlands for dinner and a movie.

Neighborhood questions

What school district is Creekside Haven in?

Montgomery ISD. Specific campus assignments are determined by the district and can change from year to year — verify the address with Montgomery ISD directly.

How far is the nearest grocery store?

About five minutes. The H-E-B on TX-105 and Kroger Marketplace are both a short drive, along with pharmacies, banks and everyday retail.

Is this a reasonable commute to The Woodlands?

Most residents get to The Woodlands in roughly 25 minutes via FM 149 and TX-249, and to Conroe or the I-45 corridor in about 20.

What is there to do nearby?

Lake Conroe for boating and lakeside dining, golf courses within a few minutes, state park trails, and the restaurants, shops and festivals of historic downtown Montgomery.

Best way to judge a neighborhood? Drive it.
