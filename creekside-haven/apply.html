How it works

Pick your home

Choose a floor plan, building and move-in date with a leasing specialist — in person or over the phone.

Apply online

One application per adult 18 and over. You will need ID, income information and your rental history.

Screening

We verify income, rental history and run a credit and background check. Most decisions land in one to two business days.

Sign & move in

Sign electronically, pay move-in costs, schedule your key pickup, and the home is yours.

What you will need

  • Government-issued photo ID for each applicant 18 or older
  • Proof of income — typically gross monthly income of at least three times the rent
  • Two most recent pay stubs, an offer letter, or two years of tax returns if self-employed
  • Rental history for the past two years, with contact information
  • Consent to a credit and criminal background screening
  • Pet details and vaccination records, if applicable

Fees & deposits

  • Application fee: $50 per applicant, non-refundable
  • Administrative fee: $150 per home, due at signing
  • Security deposit: from $300, based on screening results
  • Pet fee: $300 per pet, plus $25 per month pet rent
  • Garage / covered parking: optional monthly charge; open surface parking is included

Amounts shown are current as of publication and are subject to change. Your leasing specialist will confirm exact move-in costs in writing before you sign.

Resident selection

Clear standards, applied the same way to everyone.

Creekside Haven is an equal housing opportunity community. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, or any other class protected by federal, state or local law.

Our full resident selection criteria are available at the leasing office and are provided with every application. Reasonable accommodations for applicants and residents with disabilities are available on request.

Applying with a co-signer or guarantor

If your income does not meet the standard on its own, a qualified guarantor may apply on your behalf. Guarantors complete the same application and screening and are generally expected to show income of at least five times the monthly rent.

Corporate & relocation leases

Working with an employer or relocation service? Call the leasing office and ask for corporate leasing — we can hold a home while paperwork clears.

Application questions

How long does approval take?

Most applications are decided within one to two business days once every adult applicant has submitted their documents.

Is the application fee refundable?

The application fee covers screening costs and is non-refundable, whether or not the application is approved.

What if my credit is not perfect?

Credit is one factor among several. Applicants who do not meet the standard outright may still be approved with an additional deposit or a qualified guarantor.

Can I hold a specific home?

Yes. Once your application is approved and the administrative fee is paid, the home is held for your agreed move-in date.

Do you accept housing vouchers?

We follow all applicable federal, state and local laws regarding sources of income. Contact the leasing office to discuss your situation.

What is the pet policy?

Up to two pets per home. A pet fee and monthly pet rent apply, and weight and breed restrictions are in effect — ask for the current list before you apply.
