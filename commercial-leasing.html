Questions

Commercial leasing FAQ

What does the 48-hour swap-out guarantee cover?

If your leased machine ever needs service, we deliver a working spare within 48 hours and take yours in for repair — at no extra charge. Your business keeps pouring with no downtime.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Standard leases are month-to-month. Multi-site and discounted-rate plans may use a term agreement, which we'll always spell out clearly before you sign.

What's included in the monthly price?

The commercial-grade machine, routine maintenance and cleaning, the 48-hour swap-out guarantee, and staff training. A one-time $100 placement fee covers delivery and installation.

Do you provide the mix and alcohol?

We supply mixes and can recommend recipes; lease partners get discounted mix supply. You provide any alcohol you'd like added, or run non-alcoholic (virgin) batches. You must be 21+ to serve alcoholic beverages.

What areas do you serve?

Greater Houston and Galveston, including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Cypress, Tomball, Spring, Conroe, Pearland, and more.

Can I buy instead of lease?

Absolutely — we sell commercial machines outright, offer rent-to-own, and provide quick, easy financing on all new and used machines. Ask us and we'll structure the option that fits your business.
