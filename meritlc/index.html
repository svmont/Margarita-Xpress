Our Story

Founded where resilience meets opportunity.

Merit Living was founded in the heart of the Lone Star State — where resilience meets opportunity — on the belief that lasting success is built on collaboration, strong relationships, and a family-oriented culture. We are a forward-thinking firm specializing in the acquisition, management, and optimization of multifamily communities, dedicated to creating lasting value for investors while elevating the living experiences of the residents we serve.

While real estate is our business, our true purpose is fostering an environment where our team thrives and grows. Our foundation is built on trust, transparency, and open communication — a culture that values people as much as performance.

The meaning behind the name

Its roots are Texan: the state's name comes from the Caddo word táysha — "friends" or "allies." Merit is achievement earned through effort, never given. Living is a life of growth, character, and belonging. Together, a simple conviction: when you earn it, you truly live it.
