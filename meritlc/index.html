Our Story

Founded on partnership, built on discipline.

Merit was founded on a simple belief: lasting success is built on collaboration, strong relationships, and a family-oriented culture. Backed by an advisory board of seasoned executives, we pair institutional rigor with a genuine commitment to the residents and communities we serve.

Investment Strategy

Where we invest, and why.

We focus exclusively on multifamily communities in Sunbelt markets shaped by four durable tailwinds — the fundamentals that support occupancy, rent growth, and long-term value.

Employment growth

Markets with expanding, diversified employment bases that draw and retain a stable renter population.

Population & in-migration

Positive migration trends and household formation that steadily deepen rental demand.

Rising incomes

Growing household incomes that underpin healthy rent-to-income ratios and sustainable rent growth.

Value-add potential

Assets where hands-on management and targeted improvements unlock operational upside.
