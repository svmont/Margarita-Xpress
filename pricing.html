Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What's included with a commercial lease?

Every lease includes the machine, routine maintenance, cleaning support, staff training, and unit swaps if service is ever needed — so you're never without a working machine.

Can I apply lease payments toward buying?

Yes — ask about our rent-to-own option. We frequently structure leases so payments build toward owning the machine. Reach out and we'll tailor something that fits your business.

Do you offer financing to buy a machine?

Yes — we offer quick, easy financing on all new and used machines, so you can spread the cost of ownership instead of paying upfront. Apply through one of our partners: quick online application or First Vision Financial.

Is there a long-term contract?

Our standard leases are flexible month-to-month. Multi-site and discounted-rate plans may use a term agreement — we'll always spell out the terms clearly before you sign.

Is delivery and setup included?

Yes — delivery, setup, and (for events) pickup are included within our core greater-Houston zones. Locations farther out may incur a small travel fee, which we always quote up front.

Do I need to provide alcohol?

You provide any alcohol you'd like added (see amounts below), or run fully non-alcoholic virgin mixes. We handle the machine, mixes, and setup. You must be 21+ to serve alcoholic beverages.

How much liquor do I need to buy?

About 3 to 3.5 liters of tequila or rum per batch of margarita or bar mix. Other flavors vary depending on your choice — just ask us.

Do I have to clean the machine?

No way — that's our job. All you do is enjoy it!

How long does the mix take to freeze from startup?

About 45 minutes to an hour from room temperature.

Do you set up the machine and show us how to use it?

Yes — we handle setup and walk you through the instructions before we leave.

How many drinks will 4 gallons serve?

About 70–75 drinks in the 8–9 oz cups we provide.

When do you pick up the machine?

Usually the next day, unless other arrangements are made.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please cancel within 24 hours of your party time, or you'll still be charged full price. This gives us a chance to rent the machine to someone else and keeps us from making a trip for nothing.
