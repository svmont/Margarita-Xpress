Good to know

Pricing FAQ

Is delivery really included?

Yes — delivery, setup, and pickup are included on all rentals and leases within our core greater-Houston zones. Locations farther out may incur a small travel fee, which we always quote up front.

Do I need to provide alcohol?

You provide any alcohol you'd like added (we'll tell you how much per batch), or run fully non-alcoholic virgin mixes. We handle the machine, mixes, and setup. You must be 21+ to serve alcoholic beverages.

How far in advance should I book?

For weekend events we recommend 2–3 weeks ahead, especially in spring and summer. Need something sooner? Chat with Frosty or call us — we'll do our best to make it happen.

What's included with a commercial lease?

Every lease includes the machine, routine maintenance, cleaning support, staff training, and unit swaps if service is ever needed — so you're never without a working machine.

Can I apply a rental toward a purchase?

Ask us! We frequently run rent-to-own and lease-to-own options for businesses. Reach out and we'll structure something that fits.
