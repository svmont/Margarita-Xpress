Our story

Frozen drinks, built for business

For over 20 years, Margarita Xpress has helped Houston businesses serve great frozen drinks profitably — serving the area since 2005.

Our focus is commercial leasing and sales — putting reliable, commercial-grade machines in bars, restaurants, country clubs, and golf courses, then keeping them running with maintenance, cleaning, and our 48-hour swap-out guarantee: if a machine ever needs service, we bring a working spare so you never lose a pour. Lease with everything included, or buy outright with setup and staff training. Need a machine short-term? We rent for those too.

As we grow, we're investing in an even smoother experience: online ordering, instant quotes, and a smart assistant to answer your questions any time of day. This site is just the beginning.
